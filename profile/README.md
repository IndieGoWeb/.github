### IndieGoWeb Ltd

IndieGoWeb Ltd is a UK-based software company specializing in B2B automation services and SaaS products. We build n8n-based workflows, agentic systems, and AI integrations for small businesses.

**Key Operations & Systems:**
- **Automation:** Self-hosted n8n (production orchestrator)
- **Data:** Self-hosted Baserow (operational database)
- **Stack:** Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, Postgres
- **AI Integrations:** Gemini, Claude, ElevenLabs, Composio

**Open Source Contributions:**
All of our open source work (custom n8n nodes, utilities, MCP servers, and infrastructure boilerplates) is published and maintained under our open source organization, [whybothercoding](https://github.com/whybothercoding).
